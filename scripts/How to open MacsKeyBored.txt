How to open MacsKeyBored (test build)

1. Drag MacsKeyBored into the Applications folder next to it.

2. Open MacsKeyBored from Applications. macOS will say it can't check it
   for malicious software, because this test build isn't signed by Apple.
   Click Done (not Move to Trash).

3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message
   about MacsKeyBored, and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or
   Touch ID. You only do this once.

4. MacsKeyBored has no Dock icon: it lives in the menu bar, and setup opens
   the first time. In setup, click Grant Access and switch MacsKeyBored on
   in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. It needs this
   to see keys before macOS acts on them and to read whether a window is
   full screen. Later test builds keep this permission.

To update: quit MacsKeyBored from its menu, drag the new one into
Applications and choose Replace, then open it the same way.

To remove: quit MacsKeyBored from its menu (which gives macOS back all its
shortcuts), delete it from Applications, and delete
~/Library/Application Support/MacsKeyBored.
